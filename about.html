Our Story

In 2019, it all began with a simple walk by Mario Hinnom in his own Forest. He discovered a massive rock in the middle of the river between the two mountains of Umilag and Linac—a place often passed by and ignored by others. But for Mario, this rock held a hidden beauty: a perfect viewpoint overlooking the peaceful flow of the river below.

Driven by his passion for nature, Mario decided to bring this place to life. Working entirely alone, he cleared and prepared the area until it became the Hinnom Rock Viewpoint. Since then, he has made this place his home, living there and personally maintaining every corner of the rock by himself.
